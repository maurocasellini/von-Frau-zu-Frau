This website is still under construction. It collects as little data as possible. This page explains which data that is, what it is used for and what rights you have.

The applicable law is the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applies in Liechtenstein through the EEA Agreement, and the Liechtenstein Data Protection Act (DSG).

2. Visiting the website

When you visit the site, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the site and to protect it from misuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These server logs are deleted automatically after a short time.

Hosting provider: NEXEUM Digital Services GmbH, Vaduz, Liechtenstein. A data processing agreement is in place with the provider. If data is transferred to a country outside the EEA, this is done on the basis of an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising services and does not embed social media plugins. The fonts are hosted locally, so no data is sent to Google or other font providers.

4. News by email

If you sign up for news, we process your email address, optionally your first name, and the time of sign-up and confirmation. We use this data only to send you news about Frauenkompass.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Sign-up uses double opt-in: you first receive an email with a confirmation link. You are only added after clicking it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email or by sending us a short message. We then delete your data. To make sure we send you nothing after you unsubscribe, your email address may remain on a suppression list.

For sending we use [name and location of the newsletter service] as a data processor. Until this service is set up, sign-up takes place by email to the address above, and the details are added to the list by hand.

5. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your details to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete messages once they have been dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

Important: No medical advice is given through this website or by email. Please do not send us any health data, findings or images. Normal emails are not end-to-end encrypted.

6. Disclosure

We do not sell data and do not pass it on to third parties. Exceptions are the processors named above and legal obligations.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access to the data we hold about you
  • rectification of inaccurate data
  • erasure or restriction of processing
  • data portability
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent, with effect for the future

A short message to kontakt@frauenkompass.ch is enough.
